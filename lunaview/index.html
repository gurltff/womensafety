      How to use LunaView

      A planetarium for the Moon

      See the sky from anywhere on the Moon, at any moment

      Everything is calculated from NASA and astronomical data: the Sun, Earth with its phase, the planets, 25,000 real stars, and the real lunar ground under your feet.

      1. 1Pick a place with the 📍 Place button, or click the Moon in Whole Moon view.
      2. 2Pick a time with the 📅 Date button, or press ▶ to watch time pass.
      3. 3Look around: drag to turn, scroll to zoom, click any star, planet or Earth to learn about it.
